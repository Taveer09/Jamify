# Welcome artwork

night-drive.svg and side-a.svg are original geometric/typographic cover designs made for Jamify's welcome screen. They are decorative fictional sleeves, not Spotify albums or playable recordings. They do not change the demo music or any user playlist.

These two SVG assets are dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
